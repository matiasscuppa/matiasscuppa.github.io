Brief Personal Bio

Matias Scuppa is an Electronic Engineer specialized in marine electronics, embedded systems, radar and navigation systems.

He has professional experience in NAVCOM systems and develops embedded diagnostic platforms for marine communication systems, including NMEA0183 monitoring, FPGA-based serial analysis, AIS/SDR systems and portable testing devices.

Through Loxodrome Tech, he develops independent R&D projects focused on embedded systems, FPGA technologies, marine communications and portable diagnostic solutions.

Selected Projects

My projects combine embedded systems, marine electronics and practical diagnostic tools for field use.

  • FPGA NMEA Analyzer: Cyclone 10 FPGA platform for multi-channel serial analysis.
  • AIS + SDR Platform: AIS reception using RTL-SDR, Raspberry Pi and Signal K.
  • Embedded Diagnostics: tools for testing and troubleshooting marine communication systems.
