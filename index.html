A Bit About Me

I am an Electronic Engineer focused on marine electronics, embedded systems, radar and navigation technologies.

My work combines field experience in NAVCOM systems with hands-on development of diagnostic and monitoring tools for NMEA0183, serial communications and embedded marine platforms through independent R&D projects under Loxodrome Tech.

Currently focused on embedded development, FPGA-based analysis systems, AIS/SDR platforms, Signal K integration and AI-assisted serial data analysis.
